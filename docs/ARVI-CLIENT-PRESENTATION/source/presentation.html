What ARVI receives

  • An installable Shopify theme built on Dawn 16.0.0, the same base as the current Shopify project.
  • Editable sections for every part of the homepage, product page and boutiques, in Italian, German, French and English.
  • A maintenance guide, QA reports and before/after screenshots.

Before launch

  • Upload as an unpublished theme and review it in the Shopify editor; the live store has not been touched.
  • Test cart, checkout and payments on the store. They are not yet verified.
  • Confirm payment methods, connect critic scores to ARVI’s metafields, and plan high-resolution boutique photography.
